Free Chargeback Response Builder

Turn your notes on a disputed payment into a clean, organized rebuttal letter you can upload to Stripe, PayPal, Square or your bank as a PDF.

How it works

  1. Pick the dispute reason, such as fraudulent or unrecognized, product not received or not as described.
  2. Fill in the transaction details and the proof you have: CVV and AVS match, customer IP, login or usage count, terms accepted, delivery details and screenshots.
  3. Watch the evidence checklist fill in, then download the PDF and upload it in your processor’s dispute portal before the deadline.

Questions people ask

Is the chargeback response builder free?

Yes. There is no signup, and the PDF is built right in your browser.

Will this guarantee I win the dispute?

No. The card issuer decides every case based on the evidence. The builder helps you present the evidence you have in a clear, complete format.

Which payment processors does it work with?

Any processor or bank portal that accepts a PDF as dispute evidence, including Stripe, PayPal, Square and Authorize.net.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a document tool. For large or complex disputes, talk to a payments attorney.
